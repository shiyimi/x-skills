# 创意规划结果示例 · 拼贴海报图片 prompt

> 本文件是 §8 图片提示词规范的完整示例（`text-to-image` / `image-to-image` 的创意规划结果，对应一次生成的最终 prompt）。它演示了 [storyboard-methodology.md](storyboard-methodology.md) 中的以下规范：

| 提示词段落 | 对应规范 |
| --- | --- |
| 首句风格总领句 | §8.1 风格总领句（风格类型 + 画幅 + 美学基调） |
| COMPOSITION | §8.5 分区拼贴（百分比分区、材质、边界过渡、区域编辑） |
| TYPOGRAPHY | §8.4 多级文字版式（主标题/副标题/角标分层） |
| ACCENT | §8.6 点缀元素（视觉尺寸 + 位置 + 偏移） |
| MOOD / STYLE | §8.7 情绪类比 + 负向 no 清单收尾 |
| REFERENCE | §8.2 元素级参考绑定（精确源 + 禁止替换） |

下面是完整示例，可直接作为生成 prompt 使用。

---

Lifestyle collage poster, vertical 3:4 ratio, warm indie zine aesthetic.

COMPOSITION: The poster is split into two zones. The upper 38% is covered by a warm brown kraft paper layer with visible natural fiber texture, subtle creases, and gentle color variation from light tan to medium brown. The bottom edge of the kraft paper has an irregular hand-torn horizontal edge with rough fibrous fibers, naturally transitioning into the photo below.

LOWER PHOTO ZONE (62%): The bottom portion is the actual street-side coffee photo — a transparent plastic iced coffee cup with a clear straw, sitting on a wooden table by a window. Through the window: a sunlit street with zebra crossing stripes, warm afternoon sunlight casting soft shadows. The coffee cup sits at the lower visual center. The photo extends to all edges with full bleed. The upper sky and cluttered background of the original photo are removed and replaced by the kraft paper.

TYPOGRAPHY: Centered on the kraft paper, slightly below the middle of the kraft zone, a large Chinese title "一杯咖啡的午后" in black ink, using a vintage Song/serif typeface with a handwritten feel — elegant, slightly calligraphic, clear and readable. Below the Chinese title, in much smaller uppercase English letters with wide letter-spacing: "AFTERNOON · COFFEE · GREEN". In the upper-right corner of the kraft paper area, small black text "2026.08".

ACCENT: A small solid caramel-brown square block (about 1.5cm visual size) placed near the title as a collage decoration element, slightly offset from the text.

MOOD: Warm, relaxed, sun-drenched afternoon feeling. Like a WeChat Moments life record meets an independent coffee magazine collage poster. The kraft paper has a scanned-paper texture with slight grain and natural aging.

STYLE: Flat, collage-style, no 3D, no neon, no gradients, no commercial advertising buttons, no extra people, no additional coffee cups, no complex stickers. The overall feeling is quiet, warm, diary-like, with a gentle indie editorial aesthetic.

REFERENCE: Use the uploaded photo as the exact source for the coffee cup, straw, wooden table, street scene, zebra crossing, and sunlight in the lower portion. Do not replace the cup or change the real-life scene.
